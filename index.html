HOW TO PLAY

  • 🦟
    Mosquitoes attack! They fly in and bite our sleepy hero. Every bite sets his skin on fire and drains health.
  • 👆
    Tap or click anywhere to fire his… third leg… at that spot. Hold to keep firing.
  • 🎯
    Hold the big SHOOT button (or Space) to auto-aim at the most dangerous bug.
  • 🔥
    Kill fast to build combos. Streaks and wave clears give bonus points.
  • ⭐
    Shoot the golden mosquito to heal! Watch for armored bugs and the Queen.
  • ⌨️
    Esc / P pause  •  M mute
